# IT3010 — Empirical Research (NTNU)

Coursework for NTNU's Research-based Innovation Methodologies in Computer and
Information Science — study design, data collection, peer review, and academic
writing rather than implementation.

## Research Project — Efficiency of Panopto (with H. Elling, A. Hæstad, G. Hanssen)

A group study evaluating the usability efficiency of the Panopto e-learning
platform through a controlled user experiment: timed task-completion across
participants of varying technical experience, quantitative analysis with
hypothesis testing. Developed across staged deliverables — from initial topic
framing (COVID-19's impact on e-learning) through method, results, and a
dissemination plan.

## Peer Review Assignments

Individual peer-review work: a structured review of another group's paper
(gamification in Duolingo) against empirical-standards criteria, and a
reflection report on the peer-review process itself.

*Empirical research · experiment design · usability testing · hypothesis
testing · academic writing · peer review*
