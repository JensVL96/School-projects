# TDT4175 — Information Systems

Coursework for NTNU's Information Systems course, covering business process
modeling, IS strategy, and process improvement. The work here is BPMN
(Business Process Model and Notation) modeling of real-world processes.

## Individual BPMN Modelling Exercise

Two written process descriptions modeled as separate BPMN diagrams:

**Case 1 — Hospital Radiology Workflow.** The end-to-end radiology service
process: a physician's procedure request and paper order form, the radiologist's
indication check (accept/reject), scheduling and patient notification, the
examination and PACS image storage, and the dictated-then-transcribed report
sent back to the requesting physician. A two-participant collaboration model.

**Case 2 — Mortgage Application Process.** The multi-party BestLoans mortgage
process: client, broker, mortgage department, credit bureau, and insurance —
covering broker mandate limits, escalation to BestLoans, credit checks,
mortgage-insurance triggers above 90% loan-to-cost, and approval/rejection
flows. A five-participant collaboration model.

Each case is provided as BPMN source plus PDF and SVG exports.
